About

Fairness in AI &
Recommender Systems

I am a PhD candidate at the University of Amsterdam, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, fairness, and recommender systems. My research focuses on developing more equitable and effective recommendation algorithms through network science and graph machine learning approaches.

Previously, I completed my Master's and Bachelor's degrees in Computer Engineering at Galatasaray University (Istanbul), where I graduated first in my class. Before starting my PhD, I worked as an AI specialist at the Credit Bureau of Turkey on anomaly detection in financial networks.
